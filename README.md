##  Testing Two Models on Arabic News Articles  
### 🧠 NLP Project  

This project evaluates and compares the performance of two models **LSTM + LinearSVC** for Arabic news classification. The goal is to measure their accuracy and effectiveness in understanding and categorizing Arabic text.

**Dataset:**  
[SANAD Dataset – Arabic News Articles](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/haithemhermessi/sanad-dataset)
